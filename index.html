Frequently Asked Questions

Verifly achieves approximately 95% accuracy on common AI-generated content. However, detection accuracy can vary based on the sophistication of the AI model used to create the content, image quality, and compression. We continuously update our detection models to keep pace with new AI generators.
Currently, all plans support image detection (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF). The Power plan additionally supports audio files (MP3, WAV, M4A) and video files (MP4, MOV, WebM) up to 20 minutes in length. Maximum file size is 50MB.
Unused scans from your monthly allowance roll over to the next month, up to your plan's maximum. Starter plans can accumulate up to 50 scans, and Pro plans up to 150 scans. Power plans do not have rollover.
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. You'll retain access to your plan features until the end of your current billing period. No refunds are provided for partial months.
Your uploaded files are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored on our servers. We do not share, sell, or use your content for training purposes. See our Privacy Policy for complete details on data handling.
AI detection is not perfect and false positives/negatives can occur. The confidence score indicates our model's certainty—lower scores suggest more ambiguity. We recommend using Verifly as one data point in your verification process, not as definitive proof.
